How to interpret the results

These formulas are commonly used to assess a company’s short-term liquidity.

Current ratio

Measures the ability to cover current liabilities with all current assets, including inventory. A higher ratio generally indicates a larger short-term asset cushion.

Quick ratio

Measures the ability to cover current liabilities using cash and receivables, excluding inventory because inventory may take time to sell.
